    Artisan Outlet user manual

    Welcome to Artisan Outlet

    Artisan Outlet turns a WordPress site into an online shop for rural artisans and handloom weavers. Every craft comes with the family who made it and how it was made. Customers pay by UPI or cash on delivery, and orders, shipping and the money owed between the shop and each artisan are all kept in the plugin. No WooCommerce is needed. Its add-on, Artisan Outlet Mobile, adds a phone app that customers can install, and in which artisans run their own orders.

    What you can do with it

    ◈

    Show the craft and its story

    Photos, price and stock, the making process step by step, care, and the artisan behind each piece.

     ☺

    Introduce the artisans

    Family story, tradition timeline, generations, awards, workshop photos and video, and up to three ways to reach them.

     ₹

    Sell with UPI or COD

    Pay with a UPI app or QR code and give the UTR, or pay cash when the parcel arrives.

     ▤

    Run the orders

    Confirm payments, ship with tracking numbers, keep the customer informed, cancel and refund.

     📱

    Let artisans ship

    Artisans who ship themselves are paid directly and run their orders from the phone app.

     ⇄

    Settle up fairly

    Commission and payouts per order, each confirmed by the side that receives the money.

    Three ideas to know first

    1. One artisan per order. A cart holds one artisan’s crafts. Adding another artisan’s piece asks whether to empty the cart and start again, so each order goes from one maker to one customer.
    2. Two ways an artisan can sell. Either the website ships (customers pay the shop, which ships and later pays the artisan their share) or the artisan ships (customers pay the artisan’s own UPI ID; the artisan ships and later pays the shop its commission). See How orders work.
    3. UPI is confirmed by a person. The customer pays from any UPI app and types in the transaction reference (UTR). Whoever received the money checks their bank and taps Money received or Not received.

    Getting started

    Installing

    Install both plugins, check two WordPress settings, and put the shop on a page. Then work through Settings, add artisans and crafts.

    What you need

    • WordPress 6.2 or newer and PHP 7.4 or newer.
    • Pretty permalinks: Settings → Permalinks set to anything except “Plain”. The phone app and the craft and artisan pages need it.
    • A UPI ID for the shop. A business or merchant UPI ID is recommended: some UPI apps limit payment links and QR codes for personal IDs.

    Installing the plugins

    1. Upload Artisan Outlet. Plugins → Add New Plugin → Upload Plugin, choose artisan-outlet-2.8.0.zip, Install Now, Activate.
    2. Upload Artisan Outlet Mobile (artisan-outlet-mobile-2.6.0.zip) the same way and activate it. If the core plugin is missing or older than 2.6, the add-on shows a notice and stays switched off.
    3. Check what was created. Activation creates the order tables and two pages: Cart & Checkout (/checkout/) and Track your order (/track-order/). It also adds two roles, Store staff and Artisan, and the Artisan Outlet menu.
    4. Put the shop on a page. Create a page called “Shop” containing the shortcode [ao_outlet]. Add it, the checkout page and the tracking page to your site’s menu.
    5. Set a Site Icon in Appearance → Customize → Site Identity (block themes: Settings → General). The phone app uses it as its home-screen icon.

    Getting started

    Settings

    Artisan Outlet → Settings has seven tabs. Each tab saves on its own with Save Changes, so changing one tab never touches another.

    Store

    • Order number prefix: letters only. HK- gives order numbers such as HK-1, HK-2.
    • Send staff notifications to: the email address for new orders, payments to verify and settlements.
    • Cart & checkout page and Track order page: the two pages created on activation. Change them only if you made your own pages with [ao_checkout] and [ao_track_order].
    • Help link: the address of your help page or user manual, starting with https:// (or http://). When it is set, a Help button appears at the right of the shop’s tabs and opens it in a new browser tab. Leave it empty for no button. An address the plugin can’t accept is refused with a message and the old one is kept.
    • Help button label: the button’s text, “Help” by default.

    Payments

    • Website UPI ID: customers pay this ID for orders the website ships. It must look like name@bank, or it isn’t saved.
    • UPI payee name: shown to the customer beside the ID.
    • Cancel unpaid UPI orders after (hours): orders with no payment reference are cancelled automatically and their stock released, counted from when the order was placed. Default 48; 0 = never.
    • Cash on delivery: offered only on orders the website ships, never on orders an artisan ships. Add a COD fee and a COD maximum order value (0 = no limit) if you want.

    Shipping & commission

    • Shipping charge per order: for website-shipped orders, and the default for artisans who ship themselves (each can have their own; see Selling & payment).
    • Free shipping above: orders whose items total reaches this amount ship free, whoever ships them. 0 = never free.
    • Default website commission (%): the shop’s share of the items total, not counting shipping. It can be changed per artisan.
    • Couriers: one per line. Add a tracking address after a | with {tracking} where the tracking number goes, and customers get a Track on courier site link, for example India Post|https://www.indiapost.gov.in/…?id={tracking}.

    Artisan app

    Photos that artisans take as proof of packing and dispatch. Both switches start off: when off, the app shows no photo button and the server refuses photos for that step.

    • Packing photos and Courier docket / parcel photos: switch on the steps you want.
    • Photo size (longest side, default 1280 px) and Photo quality (default 75%): photos are shrunk on the server. In testing a 1.9 MB phone photo became about 164 KB.
    • Delete order photos after (days): counted from when the order is delivered, cancelled or returned; 0 keeps them. The order history notes the removal.
    • The tab shows how many order photos are stored now and how much space they use. Staff can always add photos from the order page.

    Shop display

    How the crafts are laid out, on the website and in the phone app alike: the card template, cards per row (a maximum; rows wrap on narrow screens), cards per page, pagination (Page numbers, Load more or none), whether each card has an Add to cart button, and the artisans’ contact icons:

    • Contact icons on cards: show each artisan’s contact and social icons on paired cards (on by default). Their page always shows them.
    • Contact icon size: 12 to 40 pixels (default 18), for cards and artisan pages, website and app. Above about 22 px, three icons may wrap onto two rows on narrow cards.

    A shortcode can override any of these for one page.

    Colours & fonts

    • Colour themes: Terracotta (the default, used in this manual), Indigo, Forest, Marigold, Peacock and Charcoal. Click one, see it in the preview, then save.
    • Colours: fine-tune any of the eleven, including Photo placeholder, which fills the photo area for crafts and artisans without a photo and shows while photos load.
    • Corner roundness, Heading style (classic serif or sans) and six font sizes: body text, page headings, craft names, prices, artisan details and buttons.

    The website shop and the phone app share these colours and sizes.

    Mobile app

    Added by Artisan Outlet Mobile. It shows the app’s address, and sets the App name, the Short name under the home-screen icon (about 12 characters), the Home headline and tagline, and the Website banner, an invitation to open the app shown to phone visitors of the website.

    The catalogue

    Adding artisans

    Add the artisan before their crafts. The artisan page is where their story lives, and where you decide how their crafts are paid for and shipped.

    The artisan list

    The artisan page

    Click Add new artisan, or an artisan’s name. Type the name as the title, write the family story in the main editor and a one-line introduction in the Excerpt box; the introduction appears on cards and lists. The portrait is the Artisan photo (featured image). Tick the craft types they practise.

    Below the story is the Artisan details box with seven tabs.

    Profile

    Village or town, district, state, the craft tradition (for example “Muga and Eri silk handloom weaving”) and languages spoken.

    Heritage

    Generations practising the craft, the year the family started, awards and recognition (one per line), and the family tradition timeline: add milestones with a year, a title, a few words and an optional photo. Drag the handle to reorder.

    Photos & video

    A YouTube or Vimeo address about the family or workshop, and the workshop & family gallery.

    Contact & social

    Up to three ways to reach the artisan, shown as small icons under their name on the craft cards and on their page, on the website and in the app. For each row choose the type and type the details:

    TypeWhat to typeTapping the icon
    PhoneA 10-digit mobile number (+91 is added), or a number with its country codeStarts a call
    WhatsAppThe sameOpens a WhatsApp chat
    EmailAn email addressOpens the mail app
    WebsiteThe full address, starting with https://Opens it in a new tab
    Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, X, LinkedInThe full address, or just the @handleOpens the profile in a new tab

    These details are public: use only numbers and accounts the artisan is happy to share. Empty a row to remove it. A row that doesn’t look right (for example an email without “@”) isn’t saved, and a message says which. The private contact phone stays on the Private notes tab.

    Selling & payment

    The most important tab: it decides who is paid and who ships for all of this artisan’s crafts.

    • The website ships: customers pay the shop (UPI or COD); the shop packs and ships, and pays the artisan their share after delivery.
    • The artisan ships: customers pay the artisan’s own UPI ID (UPI only, no COD); the artisan confirms the payment, packs and ships, then pays the shop its commission. Fill in Artisan UPI ID and Name on the UPI account. Until a valid UPI ID is saved, the website ships for this artisan.
    • Website commission %: this artisan’s rate (blank = the default).
    • Shipping charge when the artisan ships: what customers pay for shipping on this artisan’s orders.

    Commission and shipping are fixed on each order when it is placed, so changing them later doesn’t alter past orders. The tab also shows what is owed either way, with a Settle up link.

    App login

    Gives the artisan a sign-in for the artisan section of the phone app, so they can confirm payments, ship orders and confirm settlements.

    1. Tick Create a login and enter the artisan’s 10-digit mobile number, and an email if they have one (for notifications).
    2. Click Update. The mobile number and an 8-digit PIN are shown once. Give them to the artisan now, in person or by phone.
    3. To reset a forgotten PIN, tick new PIN beside the number and update again.

    Artisans who don’t ship themselves can sign in too, to follow their orders and confirm payouts.

    Private notes

    A contact person and phone, and internal notes. Never shown on the website or in the app.

    The catalogue

    Adding crafts

    Each craft has a title, a description, a main photo and the Craft details box: price and stock, the artisan, how it is made, care and more photos.

    The craft page

    • Title and description: what the piece is and why it is special.
    • Excerpt: a short description for cards and the top of the page. If empty, the start of the description is used.
    • Main photo (featured image) and Craft types.

    Price & stock

    • Price and an optional Sale price (shows a Sale tag and the old price struck through).
    • Pieces in stock: leave blank for made-to-order or unlimited. Stock goes down when an order is placed and comes back if it is cancelled. At 0 the craft shows Sold out; at five or fewer it shows Only 2 left (with the number).
    • Item code (SKU): your own reference, shown on orders.
    • Feature on the home screen: featured crafts come first in the shop and the app.

    Artisan

    Made by: choose the artisan, or Add a new artisan. The artisan decides how this craft is shipped and paid for; those marked “(ships directly)” are paid directly. Craft types ticked here are added to the artisan too.

    Making process

    Materials, technique, time taken, place of origin and a making-process video. Under Step by step, add each step with a title, a description and a photo; drag to reorder. On the website these become the How it is made tab.

    Care and more photos

    Care instructions, one per line. More photos: other angles, close-ups of the weave or texture, the piece in use.

    Craft types

    Artisan Outlet → Craft types. They become the filter chips in the shop and the app, and can start a shortcode filtered: [ao_outlet craft_type="handloom"] uses the slug.

    Block editorCraft and artisan pages use the classic editor so the details box sits right under the text. A developer can switch to the block editor with add_filter( 'ao_use_block_editor', '__return_true' );.

    Selling

    The shop on your website

    One shortcode, [ao_outlet], puts the whole shop on a page, with tabs for Crafts, Artisans, Cart and Track order. Every craft and artisan also has a page of its own.

    The shop page

    The Cart tab shows a count when the cart has something in it; Track order finds an order by its number. On other pages, a floating cart button appears at the bottom corner once the cart has something in it.

    Searching

    • Search as you type. The results update about a third of a second after the visitor stops typing, without reloading the page; the search box keeps its place. One letter on its own is ignored; clearing the box shows everything again. Search or Enter also works.
    • Every public field is searched. On the Crafts tab: name, description and introduction, materials, technique, time to make, origin, care, item code, the steps of How it is made, craft types, and the artisan’s name, place and tradition. On the Artisans tab: name, story and introduction, village, district, state, craft tradition, languages, awards, generations, the family timeline and craft types.
    • Every word must match, in any field and in any order. Capitals and accents don’t matter, and the start of a word is enough (jacq finds jacquard).
    • A line above the results says how many were found. The search works together with the craft-type chips and Load more, and the page address changes with it, so a search can be bookmarked or shared.
    • Private fields (private notes, contact person, phone, email, address, UPI ID) are never searched.

    The search keeps a small index of each craft and artisan, updated whenever one is saved. It is built automatically the first time 2.8.0 runs; on a large catalogue the first page load after updating can take a moment longer.

    The Help button

    On a phone the button shrinks to its question-mark icon so the tabs keep their space; screen readers still announce it as “Help (opens in a new tab)”.

    For one page only: [ao_outlet help="no"] hides the button, and [ao_outlet help="https://…"] opens a different address.

    Contact icons

    When an artisan has contact or social links, they appear as small round icons under the artisan on paired cards, and under the figures on the artisan’s page. Each icon is its own link; the rest of the artisan’s half of the card still opens their page.

    • Phone, WhatsApp, email and website have line icons; social networks show a short label: FB, IG, YT, X, LI. Pointing at an icon shows what it is; screen readers announce, for example, “WhatsApp of Rupa Kalita”.
    • Switch them off or change their size in Settings → Shop display, or for one page with contact_icons="no" or icon_size="24".
    • Artisans without links look exactly as before.

    A craft’s page

    Below the tabs is More by this artisan. Tabs with nothing in them are hidden. Adding #making, #care or #artisan to the page address opens that tab.

    An artisan’s page

    The other tabs are Crafts (everything they sell) and Workshop (the gallery and video). #story, #tradition and so on open a tab directly.

    Card templates

    Choose one in Settings → Shop display, or for one page with card_style="…".

    Templatecard_styleLooks like
    PairedpairedCraft on the left, its artisan on the right. The default.
    ClassicsimplePhoto, name, artisan, price.
    GalleryoverlayLarge photo with the name and price over it.
    ListlistSmall photo beside the details; compact on phones.
    StorystoryPhoto, name, a short description, price and the artisan.
    MinimalminimalPhoto, name and price only.

    Cards per row is a maximum: paired cards need about 300 px each and classic cards about 150 px, so a phone shows one paired card or two classic cards per row.

    The app inside a page

    Selling

    How customers buy

    What a customer sees from cart to delivery. The same steps work in the website shop and the phone app. No account is needed: the order is found by its number and the customer’s mobile number or email.

    The cart

    Add to cart on a card changes to Added to cart. The cart shows whose crafts these are (“From Rupa Kalita”), quantities, shipping and the total.

    One artisan per order. Adding another artisan’s piece asks: “Your cart has crafts from Rupa Kalita. Each order can contain one artisan’s crafts only. Empty the cart and add this piece?” Cancel keeps the cart as it is.

    Shipping is free once the items total reaches the free-shipping amount. The cart is kept in the browser, so it survives closing the page.

    Checkout

    1. Your details: full name, mobile number (required) and email (optional, for updates).
    2. Delivery address: house and street, area or landmark, town or village, district, state and the 6-digit PIN code.
    3. An optional note for the artisan.
    4. Payment: UPI, or Cash on delivery when it is allowed. For an artisan who ships, only UPI is offered: “This artisan ships the parcel and receives your UPI payment directly.”
    5. Place order.

    Paying by UPI

    The order page opens with two steps:

    1. Pay by UPI. The amount, the payee and their UPI ID (with Copy). On a computer there is a QR code to scan with any UPI app. On a phone there is a Pay with a UPI app button that opens Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm or BHIM with the amount filled in, and the QR code under Show QR code.
    2. Enter the payment reference. After paying, the customer copies the 12-digit UTR / UPI reference from their UPI app and clicks Submit. The same reference can’t be used on two orders.

    The order then waits for whoever received the money (the shop, or the artisan who ships) to check their bank.

    An order with no reference is cancelled automatically after the time set in Payments (48 hours by default), and its stock is released. Until a reference is given, the customer can cancel it themselves with Cancel order.

    Cash on delivery

    Tracking an order

    The order page also opens from the link in the customer’s emails. With a tracking address set for the courier, a Track on courier site link appears. Notes that staff mark Show to the customer appear among the updates.

    Emails customers get

    If they gave an email: order placed (with payment details), payment received, payment not found, shipped (with tracking), delivered, and cancelled.

    Selling

    The phone app

    Artisan Outlet Mobile serves an app at yoursite.com/craft-app/. It opens in any phone browser and can be added to the home screen, after which it starts like any other app. It works with the same crafts, cart, orders and settings as the website.

    1. App name, from Settings → Mobile app.
    2. Account: your orders, sign in, the artisan section.
    3. Headline and tagline.
    4. Search crafts.
    5. Craft types to filter by.
    6. A craft: tap for its page, or Add to cart.
    7. Its artisan: tap for their profile.
    8. Home, Crafts, Artisans, Cart, Orders. Signed-in artisans get My work.

    Opening and installing

    • Share the address yoursite.com/craft-app/, put it in your menu, or let the website banner invite phone visitors (Settings → Mobile app).
    • On Android with Chrome, an Install app button appears in the app; on an iPhone, use Safari’s Share → Add to Home Screen. The icon is your Site Icon.
    • When offline, the app says so and offers Try again. Updates to the plugins reach installed apps straight away.

    Browsing

    The search boxes on Home, Crafts and Artisans search as you type, in the same fields as the website search. The list below the box updates while the keyboard stays open; tapping Search on the keyboard closes it to show the results. Back leaves the screen rather than stepping back through each search.

    Going back is instant and keeps your place in the list; results are remembered for two minutes.

    Cancelling and refunds

    • Customers can cancel online only while the order is unpaid and has no reference.
    • Staff, and artisans who ship (“I cannot make this order”), can cancel until the parcel ships, giving a reason the customer sees.
    • The stock comes back. If money was paid, the payment becomes Refund due: whoever received it refunds the customer by UPI and records the refund UTR.
    • A settlement that was due on a cancelled or returned order is dropped.

    Who did what

    Every step is recorded in the order’s history with the time and who did it: Customer, Artisan, Store staff, Automatic, or Store staff for the artisan when staff act on an artisan-shipped order on the artisan’s behalf.

    Orders

    Running orders (staff)

    The store team works from the dashboard: see what needs doing, open the order, take the next step. Staff with the Store staff role can do everything in this chapter.

    The dashboard

    Artisan Outlet → Dashboard counts what is waiting: payments to verify, orders to pack & ship, orders waiting on artisans (artisan-shipped orders not yet done), refunds due and settlements awaiting confirmation. Click a number to see those orders. Below are this month’s paid orders, sales and commission, and the latest orders.

    The order list

    • Filters: Payments to verify, Awaiting payment, To pack & ship, On the way, Delivered, Refund due, Cancelled / returned, and one artisan.
    • Search by order number, name, phone, UTR or tracking number.
    • Each row shows whether the artisan’s orders are website ships or ships & paid directly, the payment method and reference, and the settlement.

    An order

    1. Order number and statuses, and where it was placed (website or app).
    2. Items with item codes, shipping and total.
    3. History and notes.
    4. Customer, with call and WhatsApp links and the address.
    5. Payment: confirm, or not received.
    6. Shipping: who ships, updates, cancel.
    7. Website ↔ artisan: the commission and the artisan’s share.

    Confirming a payment

    1. Open your bank or UPI app and look for the amount and the reference shown.
    2. Found it: Money received — confirm payment (with an optional note). The order becomes Confirmed · Paid and the customer is emailed.
    3. Not there: type a reason for the customer and click Not received — ask customer to check. They see the reason and can enter the reference again.

    Packing and shipping

    In the Shipping box choose the next status and click Update:

    • Packed (optional).
    • Shipped: choose the courier (required) and enter the tracking number.
    • In transit and Out for delivery, each with an optional location, as often as you like.
    • Delivered, or Returned.

    Each update can carry a note for the customer and a photo of the parcel or receipt.

    Notes

    Under the history, type a note and click Add note. Notes are internal unless Show to the customer is ticked. A note can include a photo.

    Cancelling and refunding

    In the Shipping box, type a reason (the customer sees it) and click Cancel order; this is possible until the order ships. If it was paid, the Payment box then asks you to refund the customer by UPI, then record the reference: enter the Refund UTR and click Record refund.

    Orders an artisan ships

    The artisan normally does every step in the app, and staff just watch. The same buttons are there in case the artisan can’t use the app, but use them only after confirming with the artisan by phone or WhatsApp. They are recorded as “Store staff for the artisan”.

    For refunds on these orders, the artisan refunds from their own UPI account and records the reference.

    Emails staff get

    At the address in Settings → Store: new orders, payments to verify, an artisan cancelling an order, and settlements.

    Orders

    The artisan section of the app

    Artisans sign in to the phone app with their mobile number and PIN. Those who ship themselves run their orders here, from checking the payment to delivery, and settle up with the shop. Everything is a few large buttons, in the order the work happens.

    Signing in

    The tabs are To do, On the way, Done and Money. The bottom bar gains My work.

    Working an order

    • The order shows the customer’s address with Call and WhatsApp buttons, the total and the website commission.
    • I cannot make this order cancels it with a reason the customer sees. If it was paid, the artisan refunds by UPI and enters the refund UTR.
    • Photo buttons appear only for the steps switched on in Settings → Artisan app.

    Money

    Artisans the website ships for

    Artisans with an email on their login also get emails for new orders, payments to verify and payouts to confirm.

    Money

    Settling up with artisans

    Each order fixes, at the moment it is placed, the shop’s commission and the artisan’s share. Settling up means one side paying the other and the receiving side confirming it. Nothing counts as settled until the person who got the money says so.

    The amounts

    Commission is a percentage of the items total, not counting shipping. From the example orders:

    The Settlements page

    Artisan Outlet → Settlements shows each artisan’s balances (commission owed to the website, payout owed to the artisan, amounts awaiting confirmation) and the payments waiting for confirmation. Open shows one artisan, with their orders due and history.

    Commission from an artisan who ships

    1. The artisan pays from the app (Money → Pay commission) and enters the UTR.
    2. Check your bank, then click Received — confirm, or Not received, which puts those orders back to due.
    3. If the artisan paid you some other way, open the artisan and use Record commission received instead.

    Payout to an artisan the website ships for

    1. Open the artisan on the Settlements page (or Settle up on their page). The orders due are listed and ticked, with the total.
    2. Pay the artisan by UPI or bank transfer (if they have a UPI ID, it is shown).
    3. Enter the reference and click Record payout sent. It waits for the artisan.
    4. The artisan confirms in the app (Received). If they can’t, and they confirm by phone, click Artisan confirmed by phone.

    Sales report

    Artisan Outlet → Sales report: for any date range, per artisan, paid orders (with how many were direct), units, item sales, website commission and artisan share. COD orders count once delivered; totals exclude shipping. Download CSV for a spreadsheet.

    Reference

    For developers

    Data

    • Crafts and artisans are post types ao_craft and ao_artisan, with the taxonomy ao_craft_type. Addresses: /craft/…, /artisan/…, archives /crafts/ and /artisans/.
    • Orders live in their own tables: ao_orders, ao_order_items, ao_order_events (each event records the actor and an optional photo) and ao_settlements.
    • REST namespace ao/v1; the full list of routes is at the top of includes/class-ao-rest.php.

    Hooks

    ao_notifySend SMS or WhatsApp: receives $to_type, $order, $subject, $message, $context. Hook it to your provider.
    ao_payment_methodsAdd an automatic payment gateway; call AO_Orders::confirm_payment() from its webhook.
    ao_order_created, ao_order_event, ao_settlement_created, ao_settlement_updatedActions after these happen.
    ao_craft_data, ao_order_export, ao_order_view_url, ao_order_rate_limitFilters for craft data, order data, the order page address and the order rate limit.
    ao_contact_icon, ao_contact_typesReplace the contact icons (for example with official brand icons; receives $html and $type), or add and change link types.
    ao_search_textChange what the search finds for a craft or artisan: receives the indexed $text and the $post. Call AO_Search::build_all() after changing it, to re-index everything.
    aom_app_slugChange craft-app in the app address.
    ao_use_block_editorUse the block editor for crafts and artisans.

    Help

    Troubleshooting and questions

    The app or the craft pages show “Page not found”

    Pretty permalinks are off, or WordPress hasn’t refreshed its addresses. Open Settings → Permalinks, choose any setting except “Plain”, and click Save Changes (saving refreshes them).

    The UPI ID isn’t saved

    It must look like name@bank: letters, digits, dots, hyphens or underscores, then @ and the bank handle. The page says “The UPI ID does not look right” and keeps the old one.

    Cash on delivery isn’t offered

    • The artisan ships their own orders (COD is never offered for them).
    • COD is switched off, or the order is over the COD maximum order value (Settings → Payments).

    “This transaction reference is already used on another order”

    Each UTR can be used once. The customer has probably copied an old reference; ask them to check their UPI app for the payment to this order.

    An order cancelled itself

    UPI orders with no payment reference are cancelled after the hours set in Payments (48 by default), counted from when they were placed, and their stock is released. Set it to 0 to never cancel automatically.

    An artisan forgot the PIN or can’t sign in

    On the artisan’s page, App login tab: tick new PIN and click Update. Give them the new PIN shown. The mobile number must be the one shown there.

    No photo button in the artisan section

    Packing and docket photos are off by default: switch them on in Settings → Artisan app.

    “Choose the courier before marking the order shipped”

    Pick a courier in the list. To add your own couriers, edit the list in Settings → Shipping & commission.

    Questions

    Do customers need an account?

    No. Orders are found by number plus the mobile number or email used at checkout. The app also remembers orders placed on that phone.

    Can one order contain crafts from two artisans?

    No. Each order goes from one artisan, so the customer places one order per artisan.

    Does changing an artisan’s commission change old orders?

    No. Commission and shipping are fixed on each order when it is placed.

    Can staff act for an artisan who has no smartphone?

    Yes: the order page has every button the artisan has. Confirm with the artisan by phone first; the history records it as “Store staff for the artisan”.
