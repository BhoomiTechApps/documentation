    Guide Tracker user manual

    Welcome to Guide Tracker

    Guide Tracker is a WordPress plugin for live GPS guiding. A visitor opens a page on their phone and shares their location; their guide watches them move on a satellite map, sets where they should go, and talks them in by phone. The guide can also show the client the spot in Street View and send them a link on WhatsApp, and download the route afterwards.

    What you can do with it

    Share from any phone

    Visitors start a session themselves and share their location from the browser. No app to install.

    Watch them live

    Position, accuracy, speed, distance and direction to the destination.

    Set a destination

    Click the map, search a place, or pick one of your preset places.

    Show them the spot

    Street View of any point, captured and linked on WhatsApp.

    Keep the route

    Routes are saved on the guide’s device and download as GeoJSON or KML.

    Work with weak signal

    Nothing is lost offline: the route waits on the phone and fills in later.

    How it works

    1. The client starts a session. On the client page they enter their name and phone number and choose a guide. The session starts straight away.
    2. The client shares their location. Their phone sends its position every 2 seconds while moving, through your site.
    3. The guide watches on the dashboard. New clients appear automatically in the guide’s list. The guide sets a destination and calls the client to talk them in.
    4. Either side closes the session. The route stays on the guide’s device for download.

    Getting started

    Setting it up

    Install the plugin, add your guides, give each guide a login, and share the client page. No API key is needed.

    1. Install and activate

    1. Go to Plugins → Add New → Upload Plugin, choose the zip and click Install Now, then Activate.
    2. A Guide Tracker menu appears in the dashboard.
    HTTPS is requiredPhones only share their location with sites on HTTPS. Make sure your site has an SSL certificate before clients use it.

    2. Add your guides

    Under Guide Tracker → Guides, add each guide’s name, location and phone number. Clients choose from this list, so add guides before sharing the client page. See Guides.

    3. Give each guide a login

    1. Under Users → Add New, create an account for the guide and set Role: Track Guide.
    2. Under Guide Tracker → Guides, choose that user in the guide’s WordPress login column and click Save. Each account can be linked to only one guide.
    3. Send the guide the dashboard address (?gt_view=guide). They log in and see only their own clients.

    Track Guide users stay on the front of the site: they get no admin toolbar, and the dashboard menu sends them back to the guide page, except for their Profile, where they can change their password. Instead of a login, a guide can also use a private link.

    4. Share the client page

    Give clients the client page address (?gt_view=client), for example as a QR code at the meeting point or in a booking message. You can also use the start screen (?gt_view=home), which asks “Who’s using this phone?”.

    Using your own pages (optional)

    To show Guide Tracker inside your theme, create two pages:

    ShortcodeShowsOptions
    [gt_client]The client pagetheme="dark" or "light"
    [gt_guide]The guide login and dashboardtheme="…", and align="wide" or align="full" in block themes for more room

    Any page address works: the plugin finds the pages by their shortcode. Then choose under Pages and links which addresses the links in the admin use. Both pages are sent with no-cache and no-index headers; if you use a caching plugin or CDN, exclude them as well.

    For clients

    The client page

    Clients use Guide Tracker on their phone, in the browser. They start a session, share their location, and can call their guide at any time. Nothing needs installing.

    Starting a session

    1. Open the client page, or tap I’m being guided on the start screen.
    2. Enter your name and your phone number, and select a guide.
    3. Tap Start guided session. The session starts at once; there is no approval step.

    The address now contains the session’s link. Bookmark it or open it again to come back to the same session. The guide’s name and number are at the top, with a green call button that works whether or not you are sharing.

    Sharing your location

    1. Tap Start sharing my location and allow location access when the phone asks.
    2. The status line shows Sharing your location — last sent … and the GPS accuracy.
    3. Stop sharing my location pauses it; your guide sees that you stopped.

    The first position is sent at once, then every 2 seconds while you move, and every 10 seconds when you stand still so the guide knows the link is alive. Brief low-accuracy jumps are ignored.

    Keep the page openMost phones pause location when the screen locks or you switch to another app. The page keeps the screen awake while sharing; keep it open in front. If the page is reloaded, sharing resumes by itself.

    Weak or no signal

    The client page is built for places with patchy coverage:

    • Nothing is lost. GPS works without data. Every route point is kept on the phone, even if the page is reloaded or closed, and uploads when the connection returns.
    • The status line says how many points are waiting, and Back online — uploading saved route… when it catches up.
    • Retries slow down (2 s, 4 s, … up to 60 s) so a weak signal is not hammered, and start again at once when the phone is back online.
    • Points sent twice are not duplicated, and a wrong phone clock is corrected.

    The page must have been opened once with a connection.

    Ending the session

    Tap End session and confirm. Sharing stops, any saved route uploads, and the session closes and is forgotten on the phone. If you end it offline, it finishes as soon as the phone is back online.

    When the guide closes the session, the page says Your guide ended this session, within a few seconds while sharing or about 15 seconds otherwise. Start a new session begins again.

    For guides

    The guide dashboard

    The dashboard shows your clients on a satellite map, with a side panel for the session, its live status, calling, the destination and the map. It fits the window; on a phone the panel sits under the map.

    Logging in

    Open the guide page (?gt_view=guide), or tap I’m guiding on the start screen. Tap Log in, sign in with your guide account, and the dashboard opens with only your own clients. Log out is at the top of the side panel.

    If your administrator sent you a private link instead, open it and bookmark it. It works without a login and shows only your clients. Treat it like a password.

    Choosing a client

    The list at the top of the side panel holds your sessions: 🟢 live, 🟠 last seen a while ago, ⚪ not sharing. It refreshes every 10 seconds, and a message announces new clients. Closed sessions move to a Closed group.

    Live status

    The client is a blue dot that glides between updates, with an accuracy circle, an arrow for their heading while moving, and a short trail. The Live status section shows whether they are live, the accuracy, speed, distance and direction to the destination.

    Calling the client

    Call in the Call client section dials the client’s saved number. To call another number, type it in Custom number and press Call or Enter. Calling needs a device that can place calls; on a computer, use the shown number from your phone.

    Setting the destination

    The destination is a red pin. The dashboard shows the distance and direction to it, and the client’s route leads there. Set it any of these ways:

    • Click the map where it should be. A message with Undo appears for a few seconds. Untick Click on map sets destination if you prefer to pan freely.
    • Search: type a place name and press Enter to search OpenStreetMap (results near the map come first), or paste coordinates such as 26.9887, 94.6331. Preset places from your layers appear as you type.
    • Click a preset place on the map, then Set as destination.
    • Right-click or long-press the map, or Set destination and then click.
    • Drag the red pin to move it. Clear destination removes it.

    Preset places (layers)

    Places your administrator added under Layers appear on the map with labels. In the Layers section, tick a layer to show or hide it, click its colour to change it, use Labels to turn labels on or off, and the square button to zoom to it. Your choices are remembered on this device.

    Map view and shortcuts

    The Map view section has Follow client (dragging the map turns it off), Fit all (client, destination and route), the Basemap (satellite with labels from Google, Esri satellite, or OpenStreetMap streets) and Show recorded track. Sections can be opened and closed; that is remembered too.

    KeyDoes
    FFollow the client on or off
    CFit everything into view
    SPick a spot for Street View
    EscCancel picking

    Closing a session

    Close session under the list ends it for both sides: the client’s phone stops sharing and says the session has ended. The recorded route is kept for download, and the destination can no longer be changed. Reopen session undoes it, and the client’s original link works again.

    On a phone

    On a phone the map is on top and the side panel below it; scroll down for the sections. Everything works the same, except that Street View cannot capture the view on a phone (see Street View).

    For guides

    Street View

    Show the client what the spot looks like: pick a point on the map, look around in Google Street View, capture the view, and send the client a link to it on WhatsApp.

    1. Click the person button under the zoom buttons, or press S. The pointer becomes a small walker showing the coordinates. On a phone, the map says Tap a spot for Street View.
    2. Click a spot on a road. Esc cancels.
    3. A window opens with Street View there. Turn the view to what the client should see.

    Capturing and sending

    • Capture view (Chrome and Edge on a computer, mostly Firefox): the browser asks to share this tab; choose Allow. The picture is cut to the Street View area and saved on your device (Guide Tracker/pictures/), never uploaded to the site.
    • Send location to <client> (Send on WhatsApp after a capture) opens WhatsApp with the client’s number and your Message, plus a Google Maps link that opens Street View at the spot. WhatsApp gets the link, not the picture: attach the picture with Share image where the browser offers it, or from the saved file. Download saves another copy.
    • On phones a web page cannot capture the screen, so only the location link can be sent. Take a screenshot if you want a picture.

    When the view is grey

    Without a Google API key, Street View uses Google’s keyless embed. It cannot tell whether a spot has imagery, so a grey view means there is none: pick a spot on a road. With a key set in Settings, the dashboard checks first: with no imagery within 75 m it says so and offers Pick another spot; otherwise it shows the nearest panorama, facing your spot, with its date.

    The keyless embed is unofficialGoogle could change it. If it stops working, add an API key: the official Embed API and the availability check are free.

    For guides

    Routes and saved files

    The client’s route is drawn as an orange line and saved on your device as it arrives. Download it as GeoJSON or KML.

    Recorded track

    The Recorded track section shows the distance and number of points, with:

    • GeoJSON: one line per sharing run with start and end times, distance and point times, plus the destination. For QGIS, geojson.io, Mapbox or Leaflet.
    • KML: a line per run with its time span, start and end pins and the destination. For Google Earth, My Maps or QGIS.
    • Clear: deletes the saved route on this device and any points still on the site.

    Each start-to-stop sharing run is a separate part, so gaps are not drawn as straight lines. GPS noise is filtered first: fixes worse than 50 m are skipped, and a point is kept only after moving at least 5 m.

    Saved on this device

    Routes and Street View pictures are kept on the guide’s device, not on the site. The Saved on this device section shows where:

    • Chrome or Edge on a computer: press Choose a folder… (for example in Documents). A Guide Tracker folder is made with routes/ (one file per session), pictures/ and exports/ (a copy of each download). If the browser asks for permission again later, press Reconnect.
    • Other browsers and phones: the browser’s own storage for this site.
    • Delete saved routes and pictures empties it.
    Open the dashboard within the relay windowThe site keeps route points for 48 hours by default, then removes them. Open the session within that time to collect the whole route, including anything recorded while the dashboard was closed. Routes are per device: a second computer gets only what is still on the site.

    For administrators

    Sessions

    Clients create their own sessions, so there is usually nothing to do here. Guide Tracker → Sessions lists every session and holds the plugin’s settings below it.

    ColumnWhat it is
    Label / Client, Guide, phones, DestinationEditable in place. Change them and click Save at the end of the row.
    StatusOpen, or closed by guide / closed by client.
    Route relayHow many route points are on the site, waiting for the guide’s dashboard.
    Client link, Guide viewThe client’s session link and a view-only link for the guide.

    Delete removes one session with its position and route points. Tick several (or all) and click Delete selected sessions to remove many at once.

    Creating a session by hand

    To set up a session for someone, use Create a session manually at the top of the page: the client’s name, their phone number, the guide (whose number is filled in) and, if you like, a destination as latitude and longitude. Send the client link that appears to the client.

    Links are the keyAnyone with a client link can share into that session; anyone with a guide link can see that client’s location and number. Treat both like passwords, and use automatic deletion to expire old sessions.

    For administrators

    Guides

    Guide Tracker → Guides is the list clients choose from. Add every guide here before sharing the client page.

    1. Under Add a guide, enter the Name, Location name (shown to clients, for example “Sivasagar, Assam”) and Phone number. Choose a WordPress login if the guide already has a Track Guide account.
    2. Click Add guide.

    In Existing guides, edit any field in place and click Save. Delete removes a guide from the clients’ list and stops their dashboard link; their existing sessions keep working.

    Private dashboard links

    Each guide has a private dashboard link. Opened without logging in, it shows only that guide’s clients, and new clients appear automatically. Send it to guides who do not have a login, and ask them to bookmark it. New link makes a new one and stops the old one working. These links work only while access without logging in is allowed.

    For administrators

    Map layers

    Layers are sets of preset places (gates, temples, viewpoints, meeting points, trails, zones) shown on the guide dashboard. Guides can pick any of them as the destination.

    Adding a layer

    1. Under Guide Tracker → Layers → Add a layer, give it a Name.
    2. Choose a GeoJSON file, or give a GeoJSON URL. Uploading is more reliable: another site must allow cross-origin requests.
    3. Set the Label field: the property holding each place’s name. Leave it empty to use name, Name, title or label.
    4. Choose a Colour and whether it is Shown on the map by default. Click Add layer.

    Files must be a GeoJSON FeatureCollection (or a single Feature) in WGS84 longitude and latitude, up to 5 MB and 5,000 features. Points, lines and areas all work: as a destination, a point is used as it is, an area by its centre, a line by its midpoint. Other properties, such as a type or a note, appear in the place’s popup.

    In the list, change a layer’s settings and click Save, use Replace file to upload a new version, or Delete it. Layers with more than 300 features start with labels off.

    { "type": "FeatureCollection", "features": [
  { "type": "Feature",
    "properties": { "name": "Tank east ghat", "type": "viewpoint", "note": "Steps down to the water." },
    "geometry": { "type": "Point", "coordinates": [94.6368, 26.9890] } }
] }

    For administrators

    Data and uninstalling

    Guide Tracker → Data & uninstall shows what is stored on the site, has a database cleaner, and sets what happens when the plugin is deleted.

    Database cleaner

    Each action applies to the whole site and cannot be undone. Guides’ saved routes and pictures on their devices are not affected.

    ButtonRemoves
    Remove expired route pointsRoute points older than the relay window. Also runs once a day.
    Remove leftoversPositions and route points of deleted sessions, rate-limit counters and cached page addresses. Safe any time.
    Optimize tablesNothing; reclaims disk space after large deletions.
    Delete closed sessionsClosed sessions with their positions and route points.
    Delete all route pointsEvery route point on the site.
    Delete version 3 Street View capturesPictures stored on the site by version 3 (shown only when there are any).
    Clear all tracking dataEvery session, position and route point. Guides, layers and settings are kept.
    Delete everythingAll of that plus guides, layers, settings and the Track Guide role: back to a fresh install.

    When the plugin is deleted

    Deactivating never deletes anything. Deleting the plugin from the Plugins screen does what you choose here:

    • Keep everything (default): tables and settings stay for a reinstall. Only the daily clean-up and cached page addresses are removed.
    • Clean uninstall: removes all Guide Tracker tables, settings, layer files, version 3 pictures, the Track Guide role (its users fall back to your default role) and the administrators’ dashboard capability. Add define( 'GT_DELETE_DATA_ON_UNINSTALL', true ); to wp-config.php to force it. On multisite, each site’s own choice is used.

    Privacy and limits

    • Phone numbers are stored in plain text so that call links work. Limit who can open the admin.
    • Anyone who finds the client page can start a session. A hidden spam field and a limit of 5 new sessions per 10 minutes per address are built in (filter gt_client_session_rate_limit).
    • Map tiles, place search (OpenStreetMap Nominatim, for light use) and Street View come from their providers. Everything else, including Leaflet and the font, is served from your site.
    • Developers can tune timing and filtering with the filters gt_client_interval, gt_client_heartbeat, gt_guide_poll_interval, gt_track_max_accuracy and gt_track_min_distance, and let Track Guides into the admin with gt_restrict_trackguide_admin.

    Help

    Troubleshooting

    The client’s phone does not share its location.
    The site must be on HTTPS, and the client must allow location access. If they refused, the page says how to allow it in the browser’s settings; then tap Start sharing again.
    The client’s dot keeps turning grey.
    Their phone paused location: the screen locked, they switched apps, or they are out of signal. Ask them to keep the page open in front. Their route fills in when they reconnect.
    The client list says “Waiting for clients…”.
    No session is assigned to you yet. New clients appear by themselves within about 10 seconds. If you log in as a Track Guide, check that an administrator linked your account to your guide under Guides.
    A guide sees “No access”.
    Their account does not have the Track Guide role. Change it under Users.
    A private guide link stopped working.
    Someone made a New link, or access without logging in was turned off.
    Call does nothing on my computer.
    Call links need a device that can place calls. Dial the number shown from your phone.
    Street View stays grey.
    Google has no imagery there. Pick a spot on a road, or set an API key so the dashboard finds the nearest imagery for you.
    Capture view is missing or does nothing.
    Capturing works in Chrome and Edge on a computer, and mostly in Firefox. Choose Allow when the browser asks to share the tab. Phones cannot capture; send the location link or take a screenshot.
    Part of the route is missing.
    Open the session on the dashboard within the relay window (48 hours by default). Points removed from the site after that cannot be recovered.
    The dashboard asks for the folder again.
    Browsers sometimes forget the permission. Press Reconnect in Saved on this device.
    The page looks wrong or out of date after an update.
    Purge your caching plugin or CDN, and exclude the client and guide pages from caching.
    Old /gt-app/ links.
    Version 4 has no installable app. Old links redirect to the new full-window pages; version 3 picture links go to the start screen.

    Help

    What’s new

    4.0.0 A standard WordPress plugin

    • The installable app at /gt-app/ is gone. Guide Tracker runs as the [gt_client] and [gt_guide] shortcodes and the full-window pages ?gt_view=home, client and guide; old links redirect there.
    • Recorded routes and Street View pictures are saved on the guide’s device; GeoJSON and KML are made there. The site keeps route points only for the relay window.
    • New Data & uninstall page with a database cleaner, and a fuller clean uninstall.
    • Right-click rules apply only inside Guide Tracker. The dashboard fits the window height by default.
    • Fixed: the client’s status check failed with plain permalinks.

    3.0 – 3.1 Street View

    • Street View on the dashboard, with capture and sending on WhatsApp.
    • Sign-in through the site’s own login (Ultimate Member detected), with a Guide login page URL setting.

    2.0 – 2.1 Closing sessions

    • End session for clients, Close and Reopen session for guides.
    • Leaflet bundled with the plugin.

    1.x

    • Map layers of preset places (1.9), clicking the map to set the destination (1.8), destination search (1.7), the two-column dashboard (1.6), Track Guide logins, recorded tracks and offline support.
